Honest Transparency

Questions worth asking.

Is Akashvara a medical clinic?+
No. Akashvara is an integrative wellness sanctuary dedicated solely to relaxation and somatic rest. The Multiple Wave Oscillator is not a regulated medical device, does not treat illnesses, and never replaces qualified clinical care.
Are there any contraindications?+
Yes. Out of strict caution, we cannot offer sessions to anyone with an implanted electronic medical device (pacemakers, defibrillators), individuals who are pregnant, or anyone with active seizure conditions.
What does the field feel like?+
Most feel a gentle ambient warmth, mild pleasant tingling in the extremities, and a heavy, deeply relaxed sensation similar to falling into a peaceful nap.
Do I need to undress?+
No. You stay fully clothed the whole time — just wear something comfortable. You recline in a chair; nothing is placed on you and nothing touches you.
What if I don't feel anything?+
That is completely normal and not a problem. The field is subtle by design. Many people feel mostly a deep calm rather than anything dramatic — and the thing they notice most often is simply resting better afterward.
Our promise on honesty: We will never oversell what an hour of rest can do. We offer a calm, well-held room and an honest, historical multi-wave field — nothing more, and nothing dressed up as medicine.
